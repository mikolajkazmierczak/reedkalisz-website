## Scope

The whole public website: homepage (CMS blocks), category listing with facets, product page, contact, policy pages, chrome, error state. Admin panel untouched.

## Visitor mode

Persuade. The inquiry is the conversion; the catalog is the proof.

## Audience and job

B2B buyers sourcing branded gadgets, and local clients needing print, stamps, forms and engraving. Both browse, then ask. No checkout exists.

## Constraints

Polish only. Inquiry-only. Homepage stays composed from CMS blocks. REED logo fixed. Must not regress speed on a mid-range phone.

**Claims permitted:** the live catalogue count (4700+, rendered from data, not typed); office hours pn–pt 10:00–14:00; in-house laser engraving, pad/digital print, stamps, self-copying forms; founded 2002 (user-confirmed, and worth leading with).
**Claims forbidden** (user-confirmed false or unverified): any quote-turnaround promise including "wycena w 24 h" and "odpowiadamy w 24 h"; physical samples before a run; per-technique minimums or lead times; case studies; testimonials; client names or counts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A printer's poster, not a catalog template — one paper sheet running edge to edge, flat fields of red and navy, and 2px ink rules where a softer system would reach for a shadow. Refuses the white-page grid the canon build shipped, the warm-cream ground that reads as generated, and the invented proof the reference mockup leaned on.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground (#fdfdfc) edge to edge, no mount and no island gaps — separation is carried by 2px rules and by the fields themselves; ink #111110; REED red #bf0417 for action, state and the one hero field; navy #1b2f4e for structural bands; orange #c2560b for promotions only. Bricolage Grotesque from Google Fonts, optical-size axis; platform mono for codes and tracked labels. Blocks are square and bounded by rules, controls are pills — no third shape. Zero shadows except a genuine overlay. The left rail is the whole navigation and a fixed frame, not a stack: logo and search hold the top, the category tree is the only part that scrolls, contact and the phone line pin to the bottom.

STORY: The visitor sees that REED makes the mark as well as supplying the object, narrows by category and facet, reads price-from and colourways at a glance, and sends an inquiry from beside the price.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One paper sheet, edge to edge. Left: a ruled rail carrying the REED mark at 46px, a compact search, the category tree with REED's own emoji titles intact, and Kontakt. Right: a full-bleed red field with the display line "Gadżety, które zostają na biurku." two-toned white/ink — the buyer's outcome, not the shop's craft — over a lede naming in-house engraving and print since 2002, and two actions — catalogue and contact. Beneath it, catalogue sections as ruled cells carrying real counts and real price-from. On a phone the rail collapses into a slim bar and a full-screen ink menu.

FORM: User-pinned direction. Derived from the client's own Claude Design comps, then moved off their bone ground to a paper-and-flat-field poster at the user's direction, and stripped of their fabricated proof and template tics. Supersedes the canon build of seed 3273f350.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
