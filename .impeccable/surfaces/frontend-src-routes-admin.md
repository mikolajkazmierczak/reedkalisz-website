## Scope

The whole admin panel at `/admin`: shell (rail, header), every list page, every editor, modals, the API scanner and mappings, calculations, library, login. The public website is untouched.

## Visitor mode

Operate. REED staff maintain the catalogue all day at office desks; scanability and the flow they already know outrank expression.

## Audience and job

REED staff (print and advertising, Kalisz): add and edit products, import and review supplier API products, set calculations and margins, answer inquiries.

## Constraints

Layout and flow stay as they are: no button, title or action moves (see the user's earlier modal feedback). Bricolage Grotesque stays the only face. Navy family stays the admin's colour. Dark rail around a light workspace; the workspace is a soft light, easier on the eyes than today's bright white-on-grey. Density balanced: screen space is valuable, readability is not traded for it. Polish only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The panel is REED's own carbonless job ticket. Every box is a ply of a self-copying form printed in one navy ink: ruled cells, crisp squircle corners, printed captions, a red serial number. Refuses the generic SaaS card admin (and the old dotted board with floating rounded tiles).

OWN-WORLD: A pad of forms on a soft blue-grey board (#dde2e9) marked as a very faint cutting mat (squares, every fifth line stronger, framed short of the edges); plies in soft white (#f8f9fb), copy-blue (#e8edf4) for repeated elements, yellow (#f5eed3) for optional, grey board for uneditable. One navy ink (#1b2f4e) prints every rule, caption and button; rules at 1px in navy alpha. Corners are squircles, tighter than before; the REED logo squircled to match. Captions small, tracked, navy. Section heads sit on a navy rule; lines of perforation dots separate the bars that tear off (header, editor bar). REED red prints only the serial (item codes/numbers in editor headers), danger and the current page's icon.

STORY: Staff recognise their panel instantly, read fields and tables faster because every cell is ruled and every caption printed the same way, and feel the tool belongs to a print shop.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Produkty: navy rail as the form's printed stub (logo, sections as ruled lines, the active one a board-coloured tab with a red icon, version as the form number at the foot). Header: title in navy on the board; a perforation line appears under it once the page scrolls under the bar (at rest the bar sits flush on the first box). Editor bar: title with the item code printed after it in red as the serial. Category tree and table as ruled ledgers on white plies on the board; table head on the rows' own paper, sentence case, ruled off in the ink; pagination one joined strip of ruled cells.

FORM: Carbonless job ticket (self-copying forms), #1 on my ordered list, chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the rolled Two-Ink Swatch Book; seed key 9e576766.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
